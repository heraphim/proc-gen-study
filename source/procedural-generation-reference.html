What You Can Make With Procedural Generation

Domains
Procedural generation is a spectrum, not a technique. At one end a shader invents a whole planet from a seed; at the other, a constraint solver emits a legal shift roster. Roughly half of what follows sits outside entertainment entirely — there it goes by other names: generative design, inverse design, design automation, or just optimisation. Same idea, different vocabulary and much larger budgets.
